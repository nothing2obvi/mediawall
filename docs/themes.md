## Themes

Alongside `default`, MediaWall has 20 themes: Dracula, Nord, Catppuccin Latte, Catppuccin Mocha, Gruvbox Dark, Gruvbox Light, Solarized Dark, Solarized Light, Tokyo Night, One Dark, Monokai, Rose Pine, Everforest, Kanagawa, Synthwave 84, Material Palenight, Night Owl, Ayu Mirage, GitHub Light, and Tomorrow Night.

Leave `theme` unset or use `theme: All` to choose a theme from the display or remote. The `t` key cycles through them. Your choice is shared across the space and survives refreshes.

Set a specific theme name to lock the space to it and hide the selector. Themes change interface colors, including text, buttons, dialogs, and avatar outlines. They don't change your artwork.
